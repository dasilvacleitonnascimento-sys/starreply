FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Does Google allow automated review responses?
Yes — Google's Terms of Service allow automated responses as long as they're personalized and not spam. StarReply generates unique replies for each review, which is fully compliant.
Will the responses look fake or robotic?
No. Each response references specific details from the review itself (the customer's name, what they mentioned, the rating). Every response is unique — customers can't tell the difference.
What happens with negative reviews?
Reviews with 3 stars or below are flagged for your manual review before anything is sent. You'll get an instant notification with a suggested reply — you approve, edit, or handle it yourself.
How long does setup take?
About 5 minutes. You log in with your Google account, grant access to your Business Profile, fill in a quick brand voice survey, and you're live. No technical knowledge required.
Can I edit a reply before it's sent?
Absolutely. You can run in "Approval Mode" where every reply waits for your OK, or in "Autopilot Mode" where it sends automatically. You can switch between modes anytime.
What if I'm not satisfied?
You get a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. After that, if you're not completely satisfied, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. No questions asked.
